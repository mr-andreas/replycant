# Replycant Privacy Policy

Replycant is a self-hosted photo library. The Replycant developer does not run
a hosted photo service and does not receive your library data.

## What the app accesses

- Photo library access is used to read photos, videos, and related metadata so
  the app can sync your library.
- Camera access is used only to scan setup QR codes.
- Local network access is used only to discover and connect to your Replycant
  server.

## Where your data goes

- Photos, videos, and related metadata are sent only to the server that you
  configure.
- Data is encrypted on device before upload.
- During video playback, the app may share a key for that specific file with
  your configured server so it can stream the video. The key that protects your
  whole library stays on your device.

## Data collection and tracking

- No advertising SDKs
- No behavioral tracking
- No third-party analytics
- No data sales

## Data retention and deletion

Your server operator controls retention, backups, logs, and deletion. Deleting
the app removes local app data from that device, but does not delete data from
your server or backups.
